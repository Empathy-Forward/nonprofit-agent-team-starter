# Riverbend Neighbors -- Facts (fictional organization)

**This organization does not exist.** It's a stand-in used by the starter sample task in `tasks/starter-task-nonprofit-blurb.md` so a new user can practice the Drafter -> Scout -> Gatekeeper flow without touching any real organization's real data. Any resemblance to an actual nonprofit is coincidental.

Do not edit this file as part of onboarding or personalization -- it ships exactly as written and stays that way. See the root `AGENTS.md`'s first-run onboarding section for why.

## Facts (this is the complete source -- nothing outside this list is confirmed)

- **Name:** Riverbend Neighbors
- **Founded:** 2011
- **Years operating:** approximately 15 years, as of this writing
- **Mission:** connecting neighbors in the Riverbend area with each other and with local resources, so no one in the community has to solve a hard problem alone
- **Program 1 -- Neighbor Exchange:** a volunteer-run tool- and skill-lending network; neighbors borrow tools or trade small favors (rides, minor repairs, tutoring) through a simple sign-up sheet at the community center
- **Program 2 -- Community Table:** a monthly free shared meal open to anyone in the neighborhood, held on the first Saturday of the month at the community center
- **Location:** operates out of a single community center in the fictional Riverbend neighborhood
- **Staffing:** run mostly by volunteers, with one part-time program coordinator
- **No other facts are confirmed.** In particular: no specific dollar figures, no specific number of people served, no funder names, and no board member names exist in this source. If a draft states any of these, that's a fabrication a fact-check should catch.
