## A note on copyright nuance

Copyright protects expression, not the underlying idea of "an orchestrator plus a few specialist roles, each with its own contract file." The role names, the specific task/checkpoint mechanic, the fictional sample organization, and the actual wording of every contract in this repository were independently written for this course -- none of it is copied from myPKA's own files. That means the technical trigger for CC BY-SA's ShareAlike term may not clearly attach to most of this repository's actual content, since it isn't really "Adapted Material" in the license's technical sense.

This repository attributes anyway, and licenses its original content under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms as a deliberate, independent choice -- not because the law compels it here, but because it's honest about where the underlying pattern came from, and because keeping everything under one consistent, permissive, share-alike license is simpler for anyone building on this repo in turn. If you fork or adapt this repository, you're welcome to the same reasoning: attribute what's genuinely adapted, and choose your own license terms for what's genuinely original to your fork, with a clear conscience either way.
