# Draft a 150-word blurb for Riverbend Neighbors

This is the starter sample task -- a safe, fully worked example to run through the whole Drafter -> Scout -> Gatekeeper flow before you set up a task for your own organization. Nothing here is real; see `sample-data/fictional-nonprofit-facts.md`.

## What this task is

Write a single blurb, about 150 words, introducing Riverbend Neighbors to someone who has never heard of it. The blurb should cover: what the organization does, its mission, and its two programs (Neighbor Exchange and Community Table). Use **only** the facts in `sample-data/fictional-nonprofit-facts.md` -- no outside knowledge about nonprofits in general, no invented numbers, no invented names.

## What "good enough" looks like

- Roughly 150 words (120-180 is fine; well outside that range is not).
- Every factual claim traces back to `sample-data/fictional-nonprofit-facts.md`. No dollar amounts, no "served X people," no funder or board-member names -- the source doesn't have any of those, so the blurb shouldn't either.
- Warm, plain-language tone suitable for a website "About" section or a newsletter -- not a grant application, not a press release.
- Mentions both programs by name.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent statistics, dollar figures, or a specific number of people served -- the source facts don't include any, and a blurb that states one is fabricating it.
- Do not add a call-to-action, donation link, or contact information -- not part of this brief.
- One blurb only; don't produce multiple variants unless asked.
